Back up everything

Every tool saves your progress and settings in this browser only. Save them all to one file to move them to another device or keep them safe.

Privacy: No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and no data is collected. Your settings, like light or dark mode and page options, are saved only in your own browser and never leave your device. Use “Save all progress” above to back them up to a file, and “Load all progress” to restore them on another device. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its font from Google Fonts, so those services see standard request details such as your IP address.
