Roulette Job Picker

Picks a random job for each of your daily duty roulettes. Choose which jobs you're happy to play and which roles each roulette can go to, then roll. Your choices and last roll are saved in this browser.

Your roulettes

    Jobs you can be given

    Untick a job you don't want to play, haven't unlocked or haven't levelled far enough. Jobs with dashed outlines (Bastion and Zephyr) aren't out yet, so they start unticked and their abbreviations are best guesses.

    Roulettes and their roles

    Untick a roulette to leave it out of the roll, or untick a role to stop that roulette getting jobs from it.
