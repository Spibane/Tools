Roulette Job Picker

Picks a random job for each of your daily duty roulettes. Choose which jobs you're happy to play and which roles each roulette can go to, then roll. Your choices and last roll are saved in this browser.

Your roulettes

    Default jobs

    The jobs every roulette can give you unless you set it to custom below. Untick a job you don't want to play, haven't unlocked or haven't levelled far enough. Jobs with dashed outlines (Bastion and Zephyr) aren't out yet, so they start unticked and their abbreviations are best guesses.

    Roulettes

    Untick a roulette to leave it out of the roll. Open one and tick Custom to choose its own roles and jobs instead of the defaults.
